About LIBRA

Making library registration more convenient for every student.

What is LIBRA?

LIBRA (Library Integrated Booking and Registration Assistant) is a proposed web-based library attendance and visitor management system. It uses student registration and unique QR codes to help simplify library entry and reduce manual registration queues.

Our goal

A faster check-in process, organized attendance records, and a more convenient library experience for students and library personnel.

How LIBRA Works

Four simple steps to record your library visit.

1

Student Login

Access the LIBRA portal using your registered student credentials.

2

Get Your QR Code

View the unique QR code connected to your student account.

3

Scan at the Library

Present your QR code at the designated library scanning station.

4

Successful Check-in

The system verifies the code and records your library entry.
